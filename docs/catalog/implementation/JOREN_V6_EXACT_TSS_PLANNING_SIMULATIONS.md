are closer under the versioned L1 metric than higher-utilization options. A
valid replacement RUN-026 increases the complete week to 44.776041666666666…;
it remains a swipe choice rather than replacing the distribution-first initial
optimum. BIKE-024 and SWIM-022 alternatives similarly remain within the ceiling
only when considered against the particular retained week. No sport quota,
tolerance, minimum fill, experience label or arbitrary sport-removal order is used.

Partial triathlon options keep the original .50/.30/.20 comparison, with zero
for omitted sports. Coverage precedes distance. A valid two-sport result beats any
one-sport result even if the latter uses more load. For a single-sport goal,
triathlon allocation is inapplicable and utilization/stable ties rank its options.

  alongside source meters, while the unchanged reference estimate remains separate.

- Repetition bounds apply to the complete occurrence list, including retained
  twins; distinct occurrence identities and fixed dates remain stable on edits.
- Expanded search beyond its explicit combination limit reports incomplete search,
  without a claimed optimum or arbitrary deadline increase.
- V6 source-method provenance cannot use the Realized V2 RPE fallback without a
