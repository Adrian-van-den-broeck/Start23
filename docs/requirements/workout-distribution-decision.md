# Workout distribution decision needed

Date: 2026-10-01. No new physiology or planning rule is approved by the product test.

The current `joren-planning-ruleset-2` selector searches a finite multiset of eligible catalog workouts (at most 24 occurrences). It ranks valid combinations by absolute difference from the private weekly target, then high-budget fit in Build, then **fewer occurrences**, then stable template ID order. The swipe draft fixes its target count and sport composition from that automatic selection. Prior training history supplies hours and the Start-TSS basis, not session frequency. Thus a two-session result can be selected when it matches the target more closely, or ties with a larger set. The tested athlete's exact input snapshot and catalog candidates are absent, so the exact two-session choice cannot be reconstructed here.

There is no fixed maximum percentage for one low-bucket workout. Selection checks total fit within the smallest eligible workout contribution and applies phase, sport, high-budget, injury and placement constraints. Build high-bucket contributions remain within the approved high budget. Catalog templates require positive duration or swim distance, but no general maximum session duration/distance is defined in the catalog model. Canonical activity input allows up to 1440 minutes and 1,000,000 meters; those are recording bounds, not prescription safety caps. Actual largest-workout/weekly-target ratio for Joren requires his plan snapshot and private server-side calculation; it must not be sent to mobile or public API.

To use historical frequency, collect the athlete's actual session counts by sport over a defined observation period, with missing-data and injury handling. Adding only an onboarding average creates self-report data but does not define how the selector should respond to it. A hard single-session cap needs a new versioned rule, threshold rationale, precedence with target fit and availability, and a safe outcome when no catalog combination fits. The casual “20%” suggestion supplies none of these decisions.

**Recommendation for decision:** first approve how historical session frequency should be measured and whether it is a soft preference after existing safety/target checks. This can preserve Start-TSS coefficients and progression calculations while changing only selection ranking under a new planning ruleset. Assess the resulting week-count and availability feasibility on real anonymized snapshots. Evaluate any hard maximum-single-session rule separately, because it can make approved targets infeasible and may require a reviewed progression or manual-review policy. Neither idea is implemented in this branch.
